# Model Licensing & Attribution

UACC acts as an agentic interface layer between AI models and computer operating systems via the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

## 🧠 LLM & Vision Model Dependencies
UACC does not bundle proprietary model weights. When using third-party AI models or local open-weights vision models with UACC, the following licenses apply:

- **EasyOCR / PyTorch**: Distributed under the Apache 2.0 / BSD licenses.
- **Anthropic Claude Models**: Governed by Anthropic API Terms of Service.
- **OpenAI Models**: Governed by OpenAI Terms of Use and Business Terms.
- **Google Gemini Models**: Governed by Google AI Terms of Service.
- **Qwen / Llama / DeepSeek Open-Weights**: Subject to their respective community licenses when hosted locally.

Users and developers are responsible for complying with the applicable license terms of any AI model connected to UACC.
